questions:
  - q: Which script was used to write the earliest known form of Greek?
    options:
      - Linear A
      - Linear B
      - Cretan Hieroglyphic
      - The Phaistos Disc script
    answer: 1
    explain: Linear B was adapted from Minoan Linear A to write Greek. Michael Ventris deciphered it in 1952, pushing attested Greek back roughly five centuries before Homer.
    link: linear-b
  - q: What is the strongest evidence that Mycenaean Greeks controlled Knossos after c. 1450 BC?
    options:
      - Mycenaean-style pottery found on Crete
      - The palace was rebuilt in mainland style
      - The Knossos archives switch to Linear B, which records Greek
      - Homer says so in the Iliad
    answer: 2
    explain: 'Material culture can travel through trade. Administration cannot: if the palace records are kept in Greek, Greek-speakers were running the palace.'
    link: minoan-destructions-1450
  - q: Why do Linear B tablets survive at all?
    options:
      - They were fired deliberately for permanent storage
      - They were carved in stone
      - Destruction fires baked the unfired clay
      - They were sealed in dry tombs
    answer: 2
    explain: The tablets were temporary records meant to be recycled annually. Every archive we have exists because a palace burned down — a striking case of survival bias.
    link: linear-b-tablet
  - q: 'The Mask of Agamemnon dates to approximately:'
    options:
      - 1550–1500 BC
      - 1250–1180 BC
      - 800–750 BC
      - 480–450 BC
    answer: 0
    explain: Roughly three centuries before any traditional date for the Trojan War. Schliemann's name for it was wishful, and it has proved impossible to dislodge.
    link: mask-of-agamemnon
  - q: Which is NOT supported by current evidence about the Theran eruption?
    options:
      - It buried the town of Akrotiri
      - It generated tsunamis that struck Crete
      - It directly caused the destruction of the Cretan palaces in 1450 BC
      - Its ash has been identified across the eastern Mediterranean
    answer: 2
    explain: On the high chronology the eruption is c. 1620–1600 BC — more than a century before the 1450 BC destructions. The two events cannot be directly linked.
    link: theran-eruption
  - q: What do the late Mycenaean water systems at Mycenae, Tiryns and Athens suggest?
    options:
      - A prolonged drought was already underway
      - The palaces anticipated being besieged
      - Population growth outstripped water supply
      - Religious use of underground springs
    answer: 1
    explain: Three separate citadels independently cut concealed passages to springs outside their walls, late in the period. That is siege preparation.
    link: palace-destructions-1200
