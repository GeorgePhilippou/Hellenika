      - Coins struck to commemorate the battle
    answer: 1
    explain: 'Astronomical records convert to modern dates exactly. It is one of the most securely dated events in ancient history: 1 October 331 BC.'

    answer: 1

    answer: 2

      - Cavalry superiority
    answer: 2
    explain: 'Polybius set out the analysis: the phalanx is unbeatable frontally on level ground, and helpless once gaps open. Maniples were trained to exploit exactly that.'
