  - q: 'Cleisthenes'' ten tribes were each composed of trittyes drawn from three different regions in order to:'
    options:
      - Balance the tribes by population
      - Break the link between aristocratic families and local power bases
      - Simplify military recruitment
      - Distribute farmland more fairly
    answer: 1
    explain: A tribe with members from city, coast and inland had no single local patron. It is social engineering as constitutional design.
    link: cleisthenes-reforms
  - q: What did Themistocles persuade Athens to do with the silver strike at Laurion in 483 BC?
    options:
      - Distribute it to citizens
      - Build the Parthenon
      - Build 200 triremes
      - Pay tribute to Persia
    answer: 2
    explain: Three years later that fleet fought at Salamis. It also made the poorest citizens, who rowed the ships, politically indispensable.
    link: themistocles
  - q: 'At Salamis the Greeks fought in narrow straits because:'
    options:
      - The Persians chose the ground
      - It negated the Persian numerical advantage
      - It was closer to Athens
      - The Greek ships were faster in open water
    answer: 1
    explain: In the narrows a larger fleet cannot deploy and ships foul each other. Themistocles reportedly leaked false intelligence to force the engagement there.
    link: battle-salamis
  - q: 'Moving the Delian League treasury to Athens in 454 BC mattered because:'
    options:
      - Delos was under Persian threat
      - It marked the conversion of an alliance into an empire
      - Athens needed the silver for coinage
      - The temple on Delos had burned
    answer: 1
    explain: Allied tribute then funded the Acropolis building programme. Members who tried to leave were besieged and forced back.
    link: delian-league
  - q: 'Epaminondas defeated Sparta at Leuctra by:'
    options:
      - Using cavalry to outflank the phalanx
      - Massing his left wing fifty ranks deep against the Spartan right
      - Attacking at night
      - Cutting the Spartan supply lines
    answer: 1
    explain: It inverted the convention that the best troops go on the right. Around 400 of 700 Spartiates died — a loss Sparta's demography could not absorb.
    link: battle-leuctra
  - q: Which best describes the political context of Socrates' trial?
    options:
      - Athens was at the height of its imperial power
      - Athens had recently lost the war and endured the Thirty Tyrants
      - Macedon had just taken control of Athens
      - A plague was ravaging the city
    answer: 1
    explain: Several of his associates had been prominent in the oligarchy. An amnesty barred prosecuting them for it — but not this.
    link: trial-of-socrates
