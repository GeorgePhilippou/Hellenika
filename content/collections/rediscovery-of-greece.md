<!-- field: summary -->
How Greek civilisation was saved, printed, idealised, fought over and dug up, from Byzantine scribes to the decipherment of Linear B.

<!-- field: intro -->
This collection follows Greece after the end of the ancient world: how its literature survived the fall of Byzantium, how Renaissance and modern Europe rediscovered its art, and how archaeologists and decipherers recovered what had been lost. Every stop is a moment when the ancient Greeks came back into view, and each changed what we think we know about them.
