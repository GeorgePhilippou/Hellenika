<!-- field: summary -->
A society organised entirely around war, sustained by mass enslavement — and mythologised almost beyond recovery.

<!-- field: intro -->
Sparta is the most misunderstood society in the ancient world, partly because it wanted to be. It admitted no visitors, wrote almost nothing, and left its reputation to be composed by foreign admirers writing after its decline. The archaeology and the demography tell a harder story.
