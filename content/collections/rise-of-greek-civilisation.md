<!-- field: summary -->
From marble figurines in the Cyclades to the first literate palace states of Europe — how the Aegean became a civilisation.

<!-- field: intro -->
Nothing about Greek civilisation was inevitable. It begins with islands too poor to be self-sufficient, which forced their inhabitants to become sailors, and ends three thousand years later with a language spoken from Marseille to the Indus. This route follows the first half of that: the emergence of complexity in the Aegean.
