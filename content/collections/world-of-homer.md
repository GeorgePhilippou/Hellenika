<!-- field: summary -->
How an oral tradition survived three illiterate centuries, and what it preserved along the way.

<!-- field: intro -->
The Homeric poems describe a Bronze Age that had been in ruins for four hundred years when they took their present form. Some of what they describe is accurate. Some is the poet's own world. Some is invention. Sorting them is one of the most productive problems in classical scholarship.
