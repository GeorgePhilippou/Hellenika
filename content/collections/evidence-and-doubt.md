<!-- field: summary -->
How we actually know things about antiquity — and the celebrated cases where we do not.

<!-- field: intro -->
This collection is about method rather than period. Every stop is a case where the evidence is unusually revealing about how ancient history is done: what survives, what that survival distorts, and how confident anyone is entitled to be.
