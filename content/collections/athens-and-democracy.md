<!-- field: summary -->
The most participatory government of the ancient world, and the unfree majority that made it possible.

<!-- field: intro -->
Athenian democracy was more directly participatory than any modern state, and it excluded women, foreigners and a very large enslaved population. Neither fact cancels the other. This route follows both.
