<!-- field: summary -->
Three kingdoms, one language, and the most scientifically productive period of antiquity.

<!-- field: intro -->
The Hellenistic age is routinely treated as a decline from Classical Greece. On the evidence it is the opposite: this is when Greek civilisation becomes international, professionalises science, and produces its most technically astonishing objects.
