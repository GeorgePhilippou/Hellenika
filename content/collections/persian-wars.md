<!-- field: summary -->
Two invasions, four battles, and the moment a fragmented periphery decided it was one thing.

<!-- field: intro -->
Two things should be held together here. The Greek victory was genuinely improbable and genuinely consequential. And almost everything we know comes from the winners, in a tradition that had strong reasons to make Persia look both enormous and decadent.
