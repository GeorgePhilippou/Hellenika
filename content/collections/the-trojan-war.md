<!-- field: summary -->
A case study in how to hold a story and its evidence apart without discarding either.

<!-- field: intro -->
The Trojan War is the best test of a difficult skill: reading a tradition seriously without believing it. There is real evidence for a real conflict at a real place. There is no evidence for the war of the Iliad. Both statements are true, and the interesting work happens in the gap between them.
