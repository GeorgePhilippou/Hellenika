<!-- field: summary -->
Eleven years from the Hellespont to the Indus, and the thirty-two-year-old who never lost a battle and left no heir.

<!-- field: intro -->
Alexander is easy to narrate and hard to assess. The campaign is genuinely without parallel. So is the body count, and so is the pattern of killing his own generals. Follow the route, and keep both in view.
