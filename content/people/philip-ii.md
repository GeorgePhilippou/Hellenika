<!-- field: summary -->
The king who reforged Macedon's army, subdued Greece at Chaeronea, and planned the invasion of Persia his son carried out.

<!-- field: significance -->
Built the instrument of conquest. Alexander's campaigns are inconceivable without Philip's army, treasury and diplomatic groundwork.

<!-- field: body -->
Philip II was born around 382 BC, the youngest son of King Amyntas III of Macedon, a kingdom on the northern edge of the Greek world. As a teenager he spent about three years as a hostage in Thebes, then the leading power in Greece, where he could watch the army of Epaminondas, which had just broken Sparta at Leuctra.

He took power in 359 BC, after his brother Perdiccas III was killed fighting the Illyrians, in a kingdom beaten and surrounded by enemies. He rebuilt its army from the ground up. His infantry carried the *sarissa*, a pike about twice the length of a hoplite spear, and fought in a deep phalanx; the noble Companion Cavalry became a decisive striking force; the army was paid, drilled and kept in the field year-round; and engineers built torsion catapults that could take walled cities. He lost an eye to an arrow at the siege of Methone in 354 BC.

He expanded by war, bribery and marriage — at least seven marriages, each sealing an alliance; Olympias of Epirus, mother of Alexander, was one wife among several. He took the gold mines of Mount Pangaion, destroyed Olynthus in 348 BC and steadily extended his influence into central Greece, despite the warnings of Demosthenes in Athens. In 338 BC he defeated Athens and Thebes at Chaeronea, and the next year organised the Greek states into the League of Corinth under his leadership, to invade the Persian Empire.

He never led that invasion. In 336 BC he was stabbed to death by his bodyguard Pausanias at his daughter's wedding at Aigai. Whether Pausanias acted alone, or for Olympias, Alexander or Persia, is unknown. The rich royal Tomb II found at Vergina in 1977 is most often identified as his. His army, treasury and plans made Alexander's conquests possible.
