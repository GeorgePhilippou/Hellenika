<!-- field: summary -->
An alliance against Persia founded in 478 BC that Athens converted within a generation into an empire.

<!-- field: body -->
Members contributed ships or cash to a common treasury on Delos, with Athens providing leadership. The alliance succeeded: Persia was pushed out of the Aegean.

It then became something else. States that tried to leave were besieged and forced back — Naxos in 470, Thasos in 465. Most members found it easier to pay cash than provide ships, which meant Athens built the ships and had the fleet. In 454 the treasury was moved to Athens, and tribute began funding the Parthenon. Athenian coinage, weights and measures were imposed; major legal cases were transferred to Athenian courts.

The Athenian Tribute Lists — inscribed records of the one-sixtieth of each payment dedicated to Athena — survive on stone, giving a year-by-year account of who paid what.
