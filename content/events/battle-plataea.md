<!-- field: summary -->
The largest hoplite battle ever fought ended the Persian invasion of Greece in 479 BC.

<!-- field: body -->
Some 40,000 Greek hoplites under the Spartan regent Pausanias faced Mardonius' army left behind after Salamis. After days of manoeuvring over water supplies, a confused Greek night withdrawal left the line dispersed, and Mardonius attacked what he took for a rout.

The Spartans and Tegeans held under sustained archery, then closed. In hand-to-hand fighting the Persian infantry, without comparable armour or the long spear, was broken. Mardonius was killed and the army collapsed.

On the same days the Greeks destroyed the Persian fleet's base at Mycale. The invasion was over, and the initiative passed to the Greeks — which is where the Delian League, and Athenian empire, begin.
