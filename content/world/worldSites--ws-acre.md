<!-- field: note -->
Capital of the Kingdom of Jerusalem after Saladin retook Jerusalem itself in 1187, and the last major Crusader stronghold -- its fall to the Mamluks in 1291 ended Latin Christian rule in the Levant. Crusader-era substructures survive beneath the later Ottoman old city.
