<!-- field: note -->
The first permanent Ottoman capital, captured from the Byzantines in 1326 and held until Edirne succeeded it in 1369. The tombs of the early sultans and the Green Mosque/Tomb complex survive; a UNESCO World Heritage Site as the "birthplace of the Ottoman Empire."
