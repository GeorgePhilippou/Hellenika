Capital of the Lydian kingdom under Gyges and Croesus, captured by Cyrus the Great in 546 BC and afterward a major Achaemenid satrapal seat. Excavations since 1958 have exposed a large gymnasium-synagogue complex, a Temple of Artemis and royal tumuli.
