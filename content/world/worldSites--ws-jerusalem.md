Continuously significant across Israelite, Babylonian, Persian, Roman, Byzantine, early Islamic, Crusader and Mamluk rule -- captured by the First Crusade in 1099 and made capital of the Kingdom of Jerusalem until Saladin retook it in 1187. The walled Old City contains the Temple Mount/Haram al-Sharif, the Church of the Holy Sepulchre and the Western Wall.
