<!-- field: note -->
Seat of the County of Edessa, the first and most exposed of the Crusader states, established in 1098. Its fall to the Muslim ruler of Aleppo in 1144 shocked Europe and prompted the Second Crusade. The citadel and the Pool of Abraham survive in modern Sanliurfa.
