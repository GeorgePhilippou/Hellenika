The first Islamic city founded in the Maghreb, in 670, and a base for the subsequent Arab conquest of North Africa and Iberia. Its Great Mosque, founded the same year and rebuilt several times since, is the oldest in the Maghreb; the city declined after Bedouin invasions in the 11th century.
