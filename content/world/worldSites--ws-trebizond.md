<!-- field: note -->
Capital of the Empire of Trebizond, a Byzantine successor state founded after the Fourth Crusade sacked Constantinople in 1204. It outlasted the restored Byzantine Empire itself, falling to the Ottomans in 1461, eight years after Constantinople. Its walls and the Hagia Sophia of Trebizond survive.
