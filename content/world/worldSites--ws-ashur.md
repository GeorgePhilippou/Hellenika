Political capital of the Middle Assyrian kingdom and, even after the court moved elsewhere, Assyria's religious capital -- seat of the god Ashur -- until the city was destroyed in 614 BC. Temple, ziggurat and palace ruins survive on the Tigris; a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2003.
