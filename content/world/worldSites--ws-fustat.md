Egypt's first Arab-Islamic capital, founded by the general Amr ibn al-As in 641 after the Byzantine province fell to Muslim armies. Superseded as capital in 969 when the Fatimids founded al-Qahira (Cairo) just to its north; the Mosque of Amr and the old Coptic quarter survive in what is now Old Cairo.
