A Sumerian city-state, briefly the largest city in the world under the Third Dynasty of Ur (c. 2112-2004 BC). Its stepped ziggurat, dedicated to the moon god Nanna, is the best-preserved in Mesopotamia; the royal cemetery yielded the site's richest Bronze Age finds.
