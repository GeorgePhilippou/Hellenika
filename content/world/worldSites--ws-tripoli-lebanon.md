Seat of the County of Tripoli, the last of the four Crusader states to be established (1109) and the last to fall, sacked by the Mamluks in 1289. The Crusader-era city stood nearer the coast; after its destruction the rebuilt Mamluk city (whose citadel survives) rose about 3 km inland.
