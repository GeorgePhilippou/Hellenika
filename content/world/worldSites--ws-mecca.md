A pre-Islamic sanctuary town that became Islam's holiest city after Muhammad's conquest in 630. The Kaaba, at the centre of the Masjid al-Haram, is the focal point of Muslim pilgrimage; the caliphate's political centre moved elsewhere almost immediately, to Medina and then Damascus.
