Founded in 762 by the caliph al-Mansur as a purpose-built circular capital, "the Round City" -- nothing of it survives above ground today, fully overbuilt by the later city. The Abbasid golden age centred here until the Mongol sack of 1258, after which Baghdad continued as a provincial city.
