Capital of Islamic al-Andalus, first as an independent Umayyad emirate (756, founded by a prince who escaped the Abbasid revolution) and then as a caliphate in its own right (929-1031). The Mezquita, its former congregational mosque, survives -- consecrated as a cathedral after the Christian reconquest of the city in 1236.
