<!-- field: note -->
Capital of the Western Roman Empire from 402, then of Odoacer's and the Ostrogoths' Italian kingdoms, then -- after Justinian's reconquest -- of Byzantine Italy as the seat of the Exarchate until the Lombards captured it in 751. Its Roman and Byzantine mosaics (San Vitale, Sant'Apollinare Nuovo) are the best preserved anywhere.
