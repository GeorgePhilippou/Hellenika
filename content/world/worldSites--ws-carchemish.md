<!-- field: note -->
A major Hittite, then Neo-Hittite, city on the Euphrates straddling the modern Turkey-Syria border, conquered by Sargon II in 717 BC. Its citadel and lower town reopened as an archaeological park in 2019 after decades inaccessible along the border.
