The Neo-Assyrian capital under Sennacherib and Ashurbanipal, on the Tigris opposite modern Mosul. The mounds of Kuyunjik and Nabi Yunus preserve palace and temple remains; excavation resumed in 2019 after ISIL-era destruction, and an archaeological park opened in 2023.
