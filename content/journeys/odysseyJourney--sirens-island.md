Returns to Circe, then hears the Sirens while bound to his own mast.

<!-- field: connection -->
After speaking with Tiresias, Odysseus returns to Circe to bury Elpenor and receive detailed sailing instructions. Only then does he pass the Sirens, so the line deliberately returns to Aeaea before continuing south.
