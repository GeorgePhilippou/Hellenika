<!-- field: note -->
Circe turns his men to swine, then becomes his guide to the dead.

<!-- field: connection -->
Only Odysseus’s ship escapes the Laestrygonian harbour. It sails south to Aeaea, where the crew remains with Circe for a year before she directs them toward the dead.
