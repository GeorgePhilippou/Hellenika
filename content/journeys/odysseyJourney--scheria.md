Found naked on a beach by Nausicaa; finally tells his whole story.

<!-- field: connection -->
After seven years, Calypso releases Odysseus on a raft. Poseidon wrecks it, but he swims ashore among the Phaeacians; the sweeping eastern leg represents this final solitary crossing.
