<!-- field: note -->
Giant cannibals destroy eleven of twelve ships in one ambush.

<!-- field: connection -->
The winds first carry the fleet almost to Ithaca, but the crew opens Aeolus’s bag and is blown back. After Aeolus refuses further help, the ships sail onward to the enclosed harbour of the Laestrygonians.
