<!-- field: note -->
330 BC — captures and later burns the Achaemenid ceremonial capital.

<!-- field: connection -->
From Babylon the army takes Susa, crosses the Zagros and enters the Persian heartland. Persepolis yields an enormous treasury and becomes the symbolic end of the Achaemenid court.
