<!-- field: note -->
331 BC — enters Babylon peacefully as the new king.

<!-- field: connection -->
After Gaugamela, Alexander moves south into Babylon. The city and its satrap Mazaeus surrender without a siege, giving him a major administrative and ceremonial centre.
