<!-- field: note -->
331 BC — founds Alexandria after Egypt accepts his rule.

<!-- field: connection -->
After Tyre and Gaza, Alexander enters Egypt, where Achaemenid rule is unpopular and organized resistance is limited. He founds Alexandria and visits the oracle of Ammon before marching back towards Asia.
