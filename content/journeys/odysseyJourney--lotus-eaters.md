A storm drives the fleet to a land where memory of home dissolves.

<!-- field: connection -->
After leaving Ismarus, Odysseus attempts to round Cape Malea for Ithaca. A north wind drives the ships south for nine days; the line therefore sweeps through the Aegean, below Crete, and west across open water rather than crossing mainland Greece.
