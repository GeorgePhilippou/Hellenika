331 BC — breaks the main Achaemenid army near Gaugamela.

<!-- field: connection -->
Alexander returns through the Levant, crosses the Euphrates and Tigris, and meets Darius on ground prepared for Persian chariots and cavalry. His victory destroys the last great field army defending the imperial capitals.
