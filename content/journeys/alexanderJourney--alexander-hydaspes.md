<!-- field: note -->
326 BC — defeats Porus and his war elephants in the Punjab.

<!-- field: connection -->
The long eastern leg represents years of difficult campaigning through Iran, Bactria and Sogdiana, then across the Hindu Kush into the Punjab. At the Hydaspes Alexander wins his last major battle; at the Hyphasis his army refuses to continue.
