334 BC — crosses into Asia and honours Achilles at Troy.

<!-- field: connection -->
Alexander crosses the Hellespont with the invasion army and visits the traditional tomb of Achilles before confronting Persian forces. This is a symbolic and logistical threshold rather than a territorial conquest.
