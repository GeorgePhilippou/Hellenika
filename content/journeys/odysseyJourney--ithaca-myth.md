Returns in disguise and kills the suitors besieging his house.

<!-- field: connection -->
The Phaeacians carry the sleeping Odysseus home in one of their ships and leave him on Ithaca with his gifts. This final leg is short and geographically intelligible, unlike most of the fantastic wanderings.
