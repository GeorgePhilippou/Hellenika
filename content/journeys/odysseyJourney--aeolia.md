Given the winds in a bag — opened by his own crew in sight of Ithaca.

<!-- field: connection -->
After escaping Polyphemus, the surviving fleet sails north to the floating island of Aeolus. Its placement near the Aeolian Islands is traditional, not a coordinate supplied by Homer.
