<!-- field: summary -->
A Spartan colony on the Gulf of Taranto in southern Italy, the leading Greek city of Apulia and ally of Pyrrhus against Rome.

<!-- field: significance -->
The one Spartan colony of note, and a rich city in the western Greek world.

<!-- field: body -->
Taras, now Taranto, was founded about 706 BC by Spartans, according to tradition by the *Partheniai*, sons of Spartan women born during the long Messenian war. It lay on a peninsula enclosing a large natural harbour, and grew rich on fishing, dyeing with murex purple, and pottery and coinage.

In the fourth century BC the city was ruled by Archytas, a Pythagorean mathematician who was general of Taras several times. After his time Taras repeatedly called on foreign condottieri against local tribes, including Archidamus III of Sparta and Alexander of Epirus. In 282 BC the Tarentines attacked Roman ships and appealed to Pyrrhus, who came to Italy in 280.

After Pyrrhus withdrew, Taras was captured by Rome in 272 BC. It became a Roman ally, sided with Hannibal in 212 BC, and was sacked after the Romans retook it in 209.
